Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Ullara-Eni is an attendance app that companies use to record when their staff start and finish work. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers the Ullara-Eni app at ullara-eni.vercel.app.

Who is responsible

Each company that uses Ullara-Eni decides why and how its staff's attendance is recorded, and is responsible for that data. Ullara-Eni provides the app and stores the data on the company's behalf.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, and your email address if you sign in with email or Google. Workers who use a worker ID and PIN do not give an email address.
  • Attendance records: the times you clock in and out, your shift, and whether you were on time.
  • Location: your phone's location only at the moment you clock in or out, to check you are at a work site. We do not track your location at any other time.
  • Photos: a selfie when you clock in, or a group photo taken by your site supervisor, so your company can confirm who was present.
  • Pay details: if your company uses the wage sheet, your daily wage or salary, advances and deductions.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access your other Google data.

How we use it

We use this information only to run attendance and payroll for your company: to record attendance, check you are at the right site, prepare reports and wage sheets, and keep the app secure. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described here.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own attendance history.
  • Admins at your company can see attendance, photos and pay details for their company's staff. Site supervisors can see the people at their site.
  • People at other companies cannot see your data.
  • Our service providers store the data for us: Supabase (database and photo storage) and Vercel (hosting the app). They may only use it to provide their service.

How long we keep it

Check-in photos are deleted automatically after the period your company chooses (30 days to 1 year; 90 days by default). Attendance and pay records are kept for as long as your company uses Ullara-Eni, or until your company deletes them, so it can meet its payroll and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can ask your company's admin to see, correct or delete your information. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also have rights to access, correct and erase your data and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact your company or write to us at the address below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each company's data is kept separate by rules in the database, and admins can turn on two-step login.
